The local picture

Three very different districts.

Greater Manchester isn't one commercial landscape — we print for all of its distinct pockets.

MediaCityUK, Salford Quays

Home to the BBC and a 200-acre cluster of media and creative businesses since 2010 — exactly the kind of occupier that wants a reception or breakout space that looks like it belongs to a creative industry, not a call centre.

Northern Quarter

Centred on Oldham Street between Piccadilly and Victoria stations, this is independent Manchester — fashion stores, record shops, bars and restaurants that live or die on character. A printed feature wall fits right in.

Trafford & the Trafford Centre

The UK's third-largest shopping centre anchors a huge retail and leisure district — big-brand units and independents alike, all needing interiors that can compete for footfall.
