Case Study · Spaceman Bedroom Print

A High-Resolution Spaceman Print for a Private Bedroom

← Back to portfolio

The Project

A private client wanted a high-resolution spaceman print for their bedroom: a personal feature wall with space at the heart of the design.

The Challenge

The focus was on bringing detailed spaceman artwork to the scale of a bedroom wall while keeping the image clear and the design suited to the room.

The Machine Canvas Solution

We used our vertical digital wall-printing technology to reproduce the high-resolution spaceman artwork directly onto the bedroom wall.

The Result

A spaceman feature wall that gives the bedroom its own character, bringing the client’s choice of artwork into their everyday space.
