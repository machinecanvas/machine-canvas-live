The local picture

A resort economy, and a growing one beyond it.

Blackpool is diversifying well beyond its seafront — and we print for both sides of the town.

Talbot Gateway & town centre

The £350m Talbot Gateway redevelopment by Blackpool North station is one of the biggest town-centre regeneration schemes in the North, expected to bring 8,000+ people into the town centre — new offices, retail and hospitality all needing an interior that matches the ambition.

Blackpool Airport Enterprise Zone

A key driver of the town's push to diversify beyond tourism, with new commercial space for manufacturing, logistics, digital and engineering businesses — branded reception walls and floor graphics for a new generation of Fylde Coast employers.

An extended season

The Illuminations now run from late August into early January, backed by a £4.5m modernisation programme — hospitality and attraction venues have a longer season than ever to make an interior earn its keep.
